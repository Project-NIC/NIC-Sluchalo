# NIC-Sluchalo — the requirements

*Level 2 of the project: what the tool must do, in ranges and orders of magnitude. No exact values
and no parts — a figure here is a bound for the levels below, and this page is its one home. What
it is for is in [`purpose.md`](purpose.md); the reasoning and the calculations so far are in
[`concept.md`](concept.md).*

## The walls

| wall | range |
|---|---|
| drywall on studs | a cavity of up to about 30 cm |
| solid brick, plastered | about 10 to 60 cm thick; lime-cement and gypsum plaster |
| where the things lie | in grooves a few centimetres under the plaster; anywhere in a cavity |
| concrete with rebar | as far as it goes — nothing promised |
| a damp wall | it must still work; how much worse is to be measured |

## What must show

A single installation cable and anything larger: metal pipes, steel profiles, bundles of data
cables. A thick-walled plastic conduit if it dims the field enough. Nothing is required of what
does not show.

## The picture

| | requirement |
|---|---|
| the area of one picture | small — about the size of the head, one to two tens of centimetres across |
| how it is taken | by turning the head on the spot, if that gives a picture; by sliding it otherwise (decided at level 4, by calculation) |
| a larger area | several pictures side by side |
| where a shape is | to about a centimetre |
| two things apart | told apart once several centimetres lie between them |
| depth, and what the thing is | not required; welcome if they come |
| speed | the picture grows under the hand, within a fraction of a second of the movement |
| the mains hum | a layer of the same picture: voltage on a cable, current in it; from how deep is open |
| contrast and thresholds | set by the user, per layer |

## The sender

On the far face of the wall, a few metres from it at most: a box of ours, or the Wi-Fi the building
has. Licence-free bands only, within their power limits, with no licence and no radar channels.

## The tool in the hand

| | requirement |
|---|---|
| held | in one hand; the other marks the wall with a pencil |
| display | its own; no phone, no computer needed to work |
| size and weight | a head of one to two tens of centimetres; well under a kilogram |
| running time | a few hours of scanning on one charge |
| where it works | a building site: dust, a cold shell and a hot attic, dry and damp walls; not rain |
| storing | every picture is kept with its raw data, and can be taken out to a computer |
| openness | every setting reachable; the raw data, the software and the hardware design open |

## Cost

The parts of one tool, head and sender together: thousands of Czech crowns, not tens of thousands.

## Safety

The tool may show that a cable is live; it never says that one is not.

## Open at this level

- From how deep the mains hum must show.
- How much a damp wall may cost.
