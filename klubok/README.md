# Klubok — four finders in one head

Klubok finds what runs in a wall, a floor or a board before it is touched: **the thread** — a cable
followed by the signal on it, the mains or any tracer the user already has —, **metal**, **voltage**
and **heat**. One head on the Báze (`../baze/`), held like a metal detector, one finder at a time.
Klubok is the magic ball of thread in Russian tales that rolls ahead of the hero and leads
him the way; the signal on the cable is the thread.

| finder | what it does |
|---|---|
| **the thread** | where a cable runs, by its charge and by its current — the mains at 50 Hz or a tracer's signal up to about 130 kHz, several at once; no sender of its own, so a cable shorted further on is found too |
| **metal** | near the surface, by a field of its own; iron told from copper |
| **voltage** | a sound, or a bar that grows, where the mains voltage is |
| **heat** | **Gradusnik** — the Báze's camera and thermal camera, their pictures laid over each other |

**What it is not:** not a voltage tester — it never says that a cable is dead; not a picture of the
inside of a wall — that is Rys.
