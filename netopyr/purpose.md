# Netopýr and Datel — the purpose

*A draft, 23 Nov 2026, for the owner to close.* What it does, for whom, and how we know it works.
No parts and no numbers here.

## What it does

It shows **where a gas under pressure is escaping** — steam, oxygen, argon, nitrogen, compressed
air — as a picture: the camera's view of the plant, and over it a spot where the hiss comes from.
It is held from a distance, in a hall that is loud with its own machines, and finds the leak by
the part of its sound that the hall does not make.

It has **modes, switched on the tool**, all on the same microphones:

- **The camera** — listening only: the leaks, as above.
- **The tap seen** — a surface is knocked and the picture shows which patch of it rings: plaster
  come away from the wall, a loose tile, a layer parted from what it was bonded to.
- **The tap heard** — what the woodpecker it is named after does, and what the first Datel was
  for: the tool's own knock on a sheet, a laminate or a slab, and from the echo how sound the
  material is under that spot and how deep a parting lies.

## The picture, not a verdict

As with a thermal camera, the tool does not say what is wrong; it shows where the sound comes
from and how strong it is, and a person reads it. Which gas is escaping, the pipe says, not the
tool. **No recording is kept** — what is saved is the picture, compressed, as a photograph is.

## For whom

The maintenance technician in a plant with gases on pipes — who today walks the lines with a
leak spray or an ultrasonic listener, or borrows a camera that costs as much as a car. And anyone
who wants to see the same thing for the price of a tool, built from what can be bought, with its
design open to be checked.

## How we know it works

- **A leak made on purpose** — a known opening on a known pressure — is found in the picture,
  from a working distance, while the hall runs, and the spot lies on it.
- **Two leaks side by side**, one weaker, are both seen.
- **A panel with hollows made on purpose** is tapped, and the picture marks the hollows and not
  the solid parts.
- **Against a bought acoustic camera** on the same leaks, if one can be borrowed: the same places
  found.
