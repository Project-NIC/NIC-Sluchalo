## The rangefinder's optics (25 Nov 2026)

The red-dot module in the chassis: two openings in the mask before its two lenses with a baffle
between them (or a clear window close to the lenses — never one pane across both without a
baffle), clean and unscratched; its axis parallel to the camera's, screwed to the face; the dot in
the picture calibrates the offset. Class 2 — not into the eyes.

## The air probe (25 Nov 2026)

The SHT45 in a threaded probe (M8/M12, a PTFE membrane) screwed through the case's wall on its
cable, the sensor in the outside air, away from the board's and the display's heat.

## The thermal camera's window (25 Nov 2026)

The Tiny1-C above the propeller, beside the rangefinder, its axis parallel to the camera's: an
open hole or a **germanium window** (ZnSe or chalcogenide glass also pass the long-wave infrared)
— glass and plastics are opaque to it.
