# Ariadne — why things changed

| # | what changed | why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The knock taken out of Ariadne.** Ariadne is four finders: the thread, metal, voltage, heat. | A knock suits steel and sheets — disbonds, laminates — and is of no use in masonry: a tap carries some 10–20 kHz, a wavelength of 10–30 cm in brick and plaster, so microphones above the wall draw no sharper than about half of it — where plaster is hollow, never a cable. A pulse sharp enough for a conduit must touch the wall and be heard by contact piezos, and plaster damps it within a few centimetres — another, dearer instrument. The knock lives on as Netopýr's tap mode, Datel (`../netopyr/`). |
| 2 | **A head on the Báze**, as Netopýr and Rys are; its own repository closed. The display, the box, the core board and the battery are the Báze's; heat is **Gradusnik**, the Báze's camera and thermal camera laid over each other. The requirements' display row changed from "its own, sized to the pictures" to the Báze's. | One base for every tool: the same display, box, processor and battery, so a head is all that is designed for a new tool. |
